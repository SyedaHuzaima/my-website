Nemrah Ahmed, my favourite author

Nemrah is a Pakistani novelist and author who is known for her well-written texts that explore multiple themes, including culture, society, religion, suspense, and others. Among her best works are:

Mus'haf – 2011

Jannat Kay Pattay – 2013

Namal – 2014

Haalim – 2017

Maala – 2022-2024

My favourite novels

All the works that I have read so far are my favourites, but ‘Namal’ and ‘Maala’ hold a special place in my heart for so many reasons. One of the reasons is the characterization, as the way both these novels develop and explore the themes through the complex characters is outstanding. I believe that people with a love for Urdu literature and religious and cultural themes should definitely read her novels.
